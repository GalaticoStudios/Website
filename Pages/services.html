How We Work

From first chat to launch and beyond.

  1. 1

    Talk

    Tell us what you want to make. We'll ask questions, look at what's possible and give you honest advice, even if that means a smaller scope.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    We build something playable early and fail fast, so the final product is shaped by quick iterations and real play, not months of outdated, speculative design.

  3. 3

    Build

    We develop in short iterations with regular builds and check-ins, so you always know where the project stands.

  4. 4

    We help you release and stay around afterwards with fixes, updates and support. We don't pack up the moment the deadline passes.
