Services

What we can build with you.

From full games to AR heritage trails, on-device AI and the backend that holds it all together. Bring us an idea, a half-finished project or a tricky technical problem, and we'll help you ship it.

01

Game Development & Co-Development

Complete games built from concept to launch, or an experienced team that slots into yours.

  • End-to-end development: design, prototyping, production and release
  • Co-development: we take ownership of features, levels or systems alongside your team
  • Gameplay programming, systems design and level design
  • Porting, optimisation and performance work for mobile and PC
  • Unity
  • PC
  • Mobile

Augmented Reality Apps

AR experiences that bring real places, objects and stories to life through a phone camera.

  • World-anchored AR content placed at real locations
  • Map-driven AR using Mapbox for outdoor, site-wide experiences
  • Shipped with Niantic Lightship, so we can advise on and migrate projects that relied on it
  • Built in Unity for iOS and Android
  • Unity
  • Mapbox
  • Niantic Spatial
03

Location-Based Experiences

GPS-driven games and audio tours that get people out exploring, made for heritage sites, attractions and organisations.

  • Location-based games with collecting, quests and achievements
  • Audio tours and adaptive storytelling that respond to where and how fast visitors walk
  • Experiences designed around real sites, routes and visitor flow
  • Delivered for The National Trust site Avebury, projects include Echoes of Avebury, Neolithic Trails and Tales of Flint
  • GPS
  • Mapbox
  • Audio
04

AI Integration & Tools

Practical AI features in your apps and pipelines, including language models that run entirely on your own hardware.

  • MythMaker: an AI director that turns the game mechanics a player uses into narrative as they play. It currently runs on Zork, on Windows and Android
  • Local LLM deployment in C# and .NET with LLamaSharp, so there are no third-party AI APIs and no per-request costs
  • GPU-accelerated inference with CUDA for fast responses on NVIDIA hardware
  • On-device AI for mobile that works offline and keeps user data on the phone
  • Custom AI-powered tools that speed up your team's workflow
  • LLamaSharp
  • CUDA
  • On-Device AI

How We Work

From first chat to launch and beyond.

  1. 1

    Talk

    Tell us what you want to make. We'll ask questions, look at what's possible and give you honest advice, even if that means a smaller scope.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    We build something playable early and fail fast, so the final product is shaped by quick iterations and real play, not months of outdated, speculative design.

  3. 3

    Build

    We develop in short iterations with regular builds and check-ins, so you always know where the project stands.

  4. 4

    Launch & Support

    We help you release and stay around afterwards with fixes, updates and support. We don't pack up the moment the deadline passes.
